The Case of Kurapika’s Missing Chains

Story created by Por Ter and Kyven

Just before the Yorknew Auction preview, Kurapika and the decorative chains he had brought for the exhibition went missing. Auction staff soon found Kurapika safe in a locked side room. He told them that someone had taken him there against his wishes and taken the chains. A Joker card lay outside the room. Staff later recovered the chains intact.

Find out who kidnapped Kurapika, which item they used to carry the chains and where the kidnapping happened.

Suspects

  • Chrollo Lucilfer

Evidence

At 3.15 p.m., each suspect was at a different location with a different item from the list. Exactly one of them kidnapped Kurapika and took the chains.

  1. At 3.15 p.m., footage from the Yorknew Auction shows one person leading Kurapika into the side room. The same person then comes out alone, places the chains in display box YN-12 and leaves a Joker card by the door. A tall display board hides the person’s face. Staff recovered the chains from box YN-12.
  2. At 3.15 p.m., Gon’s aunt took a photo of him carrying a fishing rod on Whale Island.
  3. At 3.15 p.m., the arrival photo at the Zoldyck family home shows Killua holding his yo-yo.
  4. A live broadcast at 3.15 p.m. shows Hisoka performing with his poker deck at Heavens Arena.
  5. The curator recognised the Joker as part of the auction’s printed invitations. A stack of the same invitations lay at the entrance for any visitor to take.

Teacher Solution

Chrollo Lucilfer kidnapped Kurapika at the Yorknew Auction and hid the chains in display box YN-12.

At 3.15 p.m., Gon was on Whale Island with the fishing rod, Killua was at the Zoldyck family home with the yo-yo, and Hisoka was at Heavens Arena with the poker deck. By elimination, Chrollo was the person shown with box YN-12 at the auction. The footage links that same person to the side room, the chains and the Joker. The Joker came from an invitation available to any visitor, so it does not identify Hisoka. Auction staff found Kurapika safe.
